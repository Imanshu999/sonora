# Credits & Acknowledgments

This project is a customized, streamlined personal fork inspired by and referencing open-source music streaming applications under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0).

### Upstream & Reference Projects
- **LastWave-Native**: Original architecture and design inspiration for native Android music streaming.
- **RiMusic / ViMusic / InnerTune**: Groundbreaking open-source Android music streaming clients using Jetpack Compose and ExoPlayer.
- **TeamNewPipe (NewPipeExtractor)**: YouTube Music and video stream extraction parser library.
- **LRCLIB**: Community-driven synchronized lyrics service and database.
- **AndroidX Media3 (ExoPlayer)**: High-performance audio player engine and MediaSession background audio handling.
- **Jamendo & Audius**: Open CC-licensed music catalog APIs and decentralized audio networks.
